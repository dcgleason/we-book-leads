Questions

Owners ask us these first

How does the free trial work?

We build your concierge on a copy of your website first, so you can try it before anything goes live. If you like it, we put it on your real site and phone line for 30 days, free. At the end you get a report: conversations, leads, bookings. If it didn't produce, you pay nothing and we take it down. You put a card on file when the trial starts so there is nothing to set up later. The card is not charged during the 30 days, and if you cancel before day 30 it is never charged at all.

Do my customers know it's an AI?

Yes, always. It introduces itself as an AI guide in its first sentence, in chat and by voice. That is a hard rule for us, not a setting.

What if it gets a question wrong?

It will, occasionally — like any new hire. That's what the tuning is for. We review transcripts, fix the knowledge base, and it improves every month. When it doesn't know, it's trained to take a name and number instead of guessing.

What do I need to provide?

Your website address and 15 minutes on a call. We pull your services, pricing and FAQs from your site, and you correct anything that's off. No developer, no new phone number, no software to learn.

What does it cost after the trial?

Monthly plans start at $297. Your exact price is set from your trial results — roughly 10% of the value of the leads it produced — so a shop getting $400 of lead value pays far less than one getting $30,000 worth. High-traffic sites with big catalogs may have a one-time setup of $1,000–$5,000. No contract, cancel anytime.

Which businesses does this work best for?

Businesses where a customer needs an answer now: HVAC, plumbing, auto repair, phone repair, dental, contractors, material handling and equipment dealers. It works less well for low-traffic sites — the trial will tell us honestly whether yours has the traffic to make it pay.
